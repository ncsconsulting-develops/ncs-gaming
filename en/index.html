The game

Our first game is out now.

No one sleeps in Hondavela. Neither do you. Rise.

A top-down pixel-art soulslike for iPhone and iPad. In the Age of Sleep, the House of the Eyelid lit pyres so their queen would never wake, and the pyres fed on those born unable to sleep. One night the Sleeper smelled the smoke and closed her eyes for good. You are one of the Sleepless: you cannot die, for to die is to sleep.

How we work

Four rules for every game we make.

  1. I

    Deliberate combat

    Every action has a cost. Fighting means reading your enemy, managing stamina and choosing the moment. Losing teaches; winning feels earned.

  2. II

    Worlds with a story

    We tell little and leave clues: in an item, in an inscription, in what a boss says as it falls. The player puts the rest together.

  3. III

    Accessible by choice

    The challenge is the one we designed, but everyone plays differently. Assistance, larger text, fewer flashes: all optional, with no penalty.

  4. IV

    Honest pricing

    Pay once and the game is yours. No ads, no in-app purchases, no required connection and none of your data collected.

About us

A small studio, made in Chile.

NCS Gaming is the video game line of NCS Consulting, a Chilean consulting firm. It is led by Nicolás Cuevas, who brings from the world of enterprise systems the habit of measuring, testing and finishing what he starts.

Vigilia de Ceniza is our first game. We made it from start to finish, with hundreds of test fights, seven languages and a map that opens up through shortcuts. We wanted a demanding game you could play on your phone without giving anything up.

And we made it as a family: Nicolás's son drew one of the bosses, the Soot-Winged. Turning that drawing into a boss fight was our favourite part of the whole project.

Nicolás CuevasDirector · NCS Gaming
